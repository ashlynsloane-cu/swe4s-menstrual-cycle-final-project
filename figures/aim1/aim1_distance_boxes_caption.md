**Aim 1 — Physiological cycle-signature distances.** Panel A compares cycles within the same study interval; Panel B compares cycles across the 2022 and 2024 intervals.

Boxes indicate interquartile ranges, black lines mark medians, and whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 × IQR of each quartile. Outliers are hidden, not excluded from the underlying calculations. Lower standardized Euclidean distances indicate greater physiological similarity. Cycle pairs are dependent because individual cycles appear in multiple pairs; the displayed pair counts are not independent sample sizes.

The exploratory mean-distance advantage is defined as the mean different-person distance minus the mean same-person distance; positive values favor within-person similarity. Brackets show Holm-adjusted p-values for two prespecified one-sided randomization contrasts. Panel A: advantage = +0.112, adjusted p = 0.2282, 25 participants, with participant labels permuted among individual cycles within each study interval. Panel B: advantage = +0.269, adjusted p = 0.2188, 14 participants observed in both intervals, with entire later-interval participant profiles reassigned as blocks.

Both tests are exploratory and require the stated exchangeability assumptions. Multiplicity adjustment does not resolve dependence or confounding. The cross-interval matched-participant sample differs from the broader within-interval cohort, so the effect sizes should not be interpreted as a direct test of temporal change. Neither adjusted p-value is below 0.05. This does not demonstrate equivalence or absence of a participant-specific effect.
